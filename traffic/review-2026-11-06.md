# Research and presentation review

Reviewed 6 November 2026 on `feature/traffic-spectral-amplification`, against checkpoint `b037ddb`. No experiment, acquisition, historical output, production page, or `main` reference was changed.

## Assessment

The project has meaningful controlled results. The strongest is nonlinear harmonic excitation: growing q=3 power occurs with decaying equilibrium q=3 dynamics, and a matched-state autonomous prediction fails to explain it. Long-wavelength concentration during absolute decay is a second useful counterexample. These are explanatory results within the tested IDM rings.

The evidence does not establish a new GFT method, a reliable warning signal, empirical instability detection, or a publishable novelty claim. Fourier/cosine equivalence on regular graphs, PSD integration, and the constructed unknown-input identity are mathematical controls. Their successful numerical checks validate implementation and interpretation, rather than establish independent discoveries. The restricted IDM fitting result is useful but combines exact equilibrium, a correct model, fewer parameters, and a different fitting objective.

## Findings and changes

1. **Research claims needed different weights.** The 8,291-word draft gave thirteen result subsections and fifteen figure embeds similar prominence. The paper now centers the harmonic mechanism, reports the negative forecast comparison directly, and places numerical/secondary grids in a separate supplement. Established algebraic identities receive prose and equations. Novelty remains unresolved.
2. **The estimator comparison changes several conditions.** TLS fits an unrestricted complex transition from adjacent snapshots; the restricted fit uses the correct IDM law, exact equilibrium, one unknown physical parameter, and whole-history fitting. The revised methods and interpretation state this explicitly. Table 1 preserves conditional control and harmonic rates without attributing the difference solely to a physics prior.
3. **Repetition was easy to misread as generalization.** For the tested homogeneous modes, within-cohort phase increments translate vehicle indices. Repeated Gaussian draws and training-noise fits perturb these fixed trajectories. The main methods and Table 1 now give this hierarchy beside the headline percentages. They supply no environmental holdout or population confidence claim.
4. **Eligibility needed method-specific denominators.** At highest noise, corrected moments have 27/80 failures and TLS 2/80; OLS has none. The former 29/320 aggregate included 80 known-operator cases. The supplement reports 29/240 learned cases and separates failure from rejection conditional on eligibility.
5. **Trace exclusion needed the analyzed-field denominator.** The last I-80 exclusion removes 94,230/243,524 acquired rows (38.69%), but 69,874/155,366 within fixed support (44.97%). It leaves one empty cell. The manuscript reports both denominators and retains the ineligible sensitivity without imputation.

No material arithmetic or unit error was found in the checked main claims. Independent review recalculated concentration/growth counts from raw arrays, harmonic slopes/rates, conditional rejection denominators, acquisition counts, and exclusion effects. The forcing identity was checked against its code and saved replay. These checks do not constitute a renewed exhaustive prior-art review or independent replication of published methods.
