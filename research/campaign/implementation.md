# Implementation choices fixed before model results

All publication-level model results follow these choices. The public article is unchanged.

- Vocabulary: adjacent alphabetic two-word phrases, minimum 20 pre-2016 abstracts, top 512 by pre-2016 document frequency after an explicit generic-word filter. H1 uses the first 128, with a 64-concept sensitivity. H2 excludes pairs sharing a token. Phrase synonyms and plurals remain separate; this limitation requires inspection of ranked examples.
- H1 predicts log(1 + monthly paper count), with an expanding ridge local baseline using a fixed penalty of 10. The correction fits causal forecast errors. Ridge correction penalties use development-period errors through 2018. Three-month forecasts are primary; one-month forecasts are a sensitivity. Graphs update at quarterly origins and use only records dated by that origin. No future exposure denominator is used. Count deviance is a secondary point-forecast diagnostic after inverse transformation, with no claim to a calibrated count distribution.
- Co-occurrence and shared-reference cosine graphs retain seven nearest neighbors per node and symmetrize their union. H1 uses degree-normalized adjacency polynomials of order two. H2 uses heat kernels at scales 0.5, 2 and 8, their pairwise distances, and their changes over 24 months.
- H2 controls include marginal support, recent activity, growth, concept age, degree, neighborhood growth, historical and recent pair counts, training-only word-context cosine similarity, common neighbors, Adamic-Adar, weighted common neighbors and preferential attachment. Models are logistic ridge (C = 0.1, 1, 10) and bounded boosted trees (depth 3 or 6; 100 iterations). Each feature set chooses its own model by 2018-2019 validation AP; the selection remains fixed for later refits. Previous complete windows become training data.
- H2 author independence is an exact existence test over triples of nonempty author sets. Each pair's future papers must also occupy at least two six-month bins. This establishes disjoint listed authors, with identity-resolution caveats.
- Ten activity-stratified graph label permutations are a bounded specificity diagnostic. They preserve the graph and permute concept labels within activity quartiles. This control does not preserve each concept's degree or historical semantic neighborhood. It supplies no causal identification or exact population p-value.
- The arXiv cs.RO harvest is a second source with overlapping scientific papers. It tests sensitivity to corpus and extraction choices. It is not an independent replication sample. Current abstracts and normalized author names remain limitations.
