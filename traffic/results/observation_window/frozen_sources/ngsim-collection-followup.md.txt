# NGSIM acquisition selection

Recorded after reading official schema and location/time-range metadata, before collecting or inspecting speeds in either selected block. The official SODA metadata endpoint is `https://data.transportation.gov/api/views/8ect-6jqj.json`. The dataset declares license identifier `CC_30_BY_SA`; physical inputs remain local and derived outputs retain attribution.

Choose locations `us-101` and `i-80`, the two freeway locations in the metadata. For each, start 60 s after its earliest global timestamp and acquire the next 180 s, with a half-open time interval. This avoids choosing an interval by its oscillation strength. Both chosen locations have epoch-millisecond timestamps consistent with the official definition. Another location in the combined dataset has a different timestamp magnitude; no combined cross-location time interpretation is assumed.

Collect selected columns for all vehicles and lanes in each interval. Retrieve a separate server-side count first, require fewer than 500,000 rows per block, and compare the downloaded row count exactly. Bound each CSV at 64 MiB, each metadata response at 2 MiB, and the aggregate at 150 MiB. Save query URLs, schema, timestamps, checksums, and failed requests. The server is a mutable public API; this is a dated hashed snapshot, not an immutable upstream release.

Retrospective figures use 5 s by 50 m bins of mean observed speed across all lanes. Select the longest contiguous spatial strip with observations in every time bin using counts alone, with a minimum eight spatial bins. Repeat at 25 and 100 m as resolution sensitivities, retaining ineligible settings. No missing-cell interpolation. Short windows, cross-lane aggregation, boundary/vehicle-selection effects, and unknown smoothing prevent a prospective or physical growth claim. Use an open path Laplacian; report its ordinary-cosine equivalence and spatial demeaning. This field measures the mean of trajectory observations in each bin, without an Edie flow/density interpretation.

TGSIM moving-trajectory metadata were also inspected. Its coordinates and speeds explicitly carry Kalman-filtered fields, with four run identifiers and separate geometry attachments. Full smoothing/geometry provenance needs another audit. No TGSIM predictive score or spectral result is authorized by metadata access alone; it remains an alternative candidate.
